# Adding your @csumb.edu email to an existing GitHub account

<!-- MAINTAINER: the organization name appears 6 times below and changes every
     term. Current: cst338-fa26ol (CST 338 online, Fall-B 2026). Search and
     replace it when the term rolls over. -->

**Why this matters:** Your instructor invited you to the course GitHub organization
(`cst338-fa26ol`) using your **@csumb.edu** email address. If your GitHub account
is set up with a different email (a personal Gmail, etc.), GitHub won't connect the
invite to your account, and you may not be able to see your assignment repos.

Adding your school email to your existing account fixes this. **You do not need a
second GitHub account.** One account can hold multiple email addresses.

---

## Before you start

- You only need to do this **once**.
- Keep your current GitHub account, do **not** make a new one.
- Have access to your CSUMB email inbox (to click a verification link).

---

## Step-by-step

1. **Sign in** to GitHub at <https://github.com> with the account you already use.

2. Click your **profile photo** in the top-right corner, then click **Settings**.

3. In the left sidebar, click **Emails**.
   (Direct link: <https://github.com/settings/emails>)

4. Under **Add email address**, type your full school email:
   `yourname@csumb.edu`

5. Click **Add**.

6. GitHub sends a **verification email** to your CSUMB inbox.
   - Open your CSUMB email (<https://mail.google.com> or the CSUMB webmail).
   - Find the message from GitHub (check Spam/Junk if you don't see it).
   - Click **Verify email address** in that message.

7. Back on the GitHub Emails page, your `@csumb.edu` address should now show as
   **Verified**.

---

## Now accept the org invitation

Once your school email is verified:

1. Check your CSUMB inbox for an email from GitHub titled something like
   **"You've been invited to join cst338-fa26ol"** and click **Join**.

   *No invite email, or it expired?* Go straight to:
   <https://github.com/orgs/cst338-fa26ol/invitation>
   and click **Join cst338-fa26ol**. (You must be signed in to GitHub first.)

2. After joining, confirm you can see the organization at
   <https://github.com/cst338-fa26ol>.

---

## Optional but recommended

- **Don't remove** your personal email, just having the school email added and
  verified is enough.
- You can leave your personal email as your **primary** address; the school email
  only needs to be *present and verified* for the org invite to attach.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "This email is already taken / in use" | The address is attached to a *different* GitHub account. Sign into that account instead, or contact the instructor. |
| Verification email never arrives | Check Spam/Junk; wait a few minutes; click **Resend verification email** on the Emails page. |
| Invite link says "expired" or "not found" | Ask the instructor to re-send the invitation, then revisit <https://github.com/orgs/cst338-fa26ol/invitation>. |
| Can see the org but not your repo | You're a member but not yet added to the repo/team, message the instructor with your **GitHub username**. |
